CalmShift: nursing brain sheets for every unit

Updated 2026-10-26 · CalmShift by KitWise Studio

CalmShift is a line of printable and fillable PDF nursing brain sheets by KitWise Studio. There is a sheet for each unit (ICU, med-surg, ER, L&D, mother-baby, NICU, peds, psych, OR/PACU, charge, home health) plus a free SBAR report sheet. Each one is an instant download you can print or fill on a tablet.

What makes them different

  • Unit-specific layouts. An ICU sheet needs drips and lines; a med-surg sheet needs room for 4 to 5 patients. One generic sheet does neither well.
  • Print or fill on screen. Every PDF has fillable fields, so the same file works on paper or on a tablet.
  • SBAR built in. Most sheets include an SBAR block so your report follows the same order as your notes.
  • Made with input from a working nurse, not a generic template shop.

Quick answers

Are CalmShift sheets printable or fillable?

Both. Every CalmShift PDF can be printed on US Letter paper or filled in on a tablet or computer with any PDF reader.

Is there a free CalmShift sheet?

Yes. The SBAR Nursing Report Sheet with provider call notes is free on Gumroad.

Where can I buy CalmShift brain sheets?

On Gumroad (kitwisestudio.gumroad.com) and on Etsy in the KitWiseStudios shop. Files are delivered instantly as a download.
