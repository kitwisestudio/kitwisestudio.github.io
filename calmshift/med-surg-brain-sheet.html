Med-surg brain sheet for 4 to 6 patients

Updated 2026-10-29 · CalmShift by KitWise Studio

A med-surg brain sheet fits 4 to 6 patients on one page with a compact box per patient (diagnosis, code status, allergies, IV, diet, activity, meds due) and a shared hourly timeline so you can cluster care. CalmShift's Med-Surg Brain Sheets include a 4-patient and a 1-patient version plus SBAR.

How to use it on shift

  1. Fill one box per patient during report.
  2. Copy every scheduled med, assessment and lab draw onto the hourly timeline.
  3. Cluster care: group tasks that fall in the same hour and room.
  4. Highlight anything time-critical (insulin, antibiotics, pre-op checklists).
  5. Give report in SBAR order straight from each box.

When to use the 1-patient version

For a complex or unstable patient, a post-op day 0, or while you are still on orientation and want room for more detail.
