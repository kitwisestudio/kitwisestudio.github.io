ICU nurse brain sheet: what to include

Updated 2026-10-27 · CalmShift by KitWise Studio

An ICU brain sheet is built for 1 to 2 patients and organized by body system, with dedicated space for drips and titrations, lines and drains, ventilator settings, hourly vitals and intake and output. It ends with an SBAR block so handoff follows the same order. CalmShift's 2-patient ICU brain sheet uses this layout.

Checklist for an ICU brain sheet

  1. Header: room, age, code status, allergies, isolation, admitting diagnosis, key history.
  2. Neuro: level of consciousness and sedation scores your unit uses, pupils, pain.
  3. Cardiovascular: rhythm, hemodynamics, pressors and their current rates.
  4. Respiratory: airway, oxygen or ventilator mode and settings, last blood gas.
  5. GI/GU and renal: diet or tube feeds, output, drains, dialysis.
  6. Skin and lines: central and arterial lines, IV sites, wounds, turning schedule.
  7. Drips table: drug, concentration, current rate, titration notes.
  8. Hourly grid: vitals, labs, meds and procedures by hour.
  9. I&O totals and an SBAR box for report.

Organization tool only. Use your unit's scales, parameters and protocols.

Quick answers

How many patients does an ICU brain sheet cover?

Usually 1 or 2, because ICU nurse-to-patient ratios are typically 1:1 or 1:2.

What makes an ICU brain sheet different from med-surg?

More space per patient: a full systems assessment, a drips and titration table, lines, ventilator settings and hourly intake and output.
