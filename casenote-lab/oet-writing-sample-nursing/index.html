OET Writing sample for nurses: Hip replacement – discharge to district nurse

This is an original practice case in the style of the OET Writing sub-test for nurses. Read the notes, decide what the reader needs, write your letter, then compare it with the model below. You can also write it in the checker and see which key notes you used or missed.

Case notes

Writing task. Using the information in the case notes, write a letter to Ms Karen Whitlock, District Nurse at Thornbury Community Nursing Team, who will be visiting Mr Hallam at home after his discharge today. Address the letter to Ms Karen Whitlock, District Nurse, Thornbury Community Nursing Team, 4 Orchard Lane, Thornbury. In your answer: expand the relevant notes into complete sentences; do not use note form; use letter format. The body of the letter should be approximately 180–200 words.

Model letter

Ms Karen Whitlock District Nurse Thornbury Community Nursing Team 4 Orchard Lane Thornbury 14 March 2026 Dear Ms Whitlock, Re: Mr Geoffrey Hallam, DOB 03/07/1951 I am writing to request your ongoing care of Mr Hallam, who is being discharged home today following an elective right total hip replacement on 9 March. He will require wound review, clip removal and support with his anticoagulant injections. Mr Hallam has type 2 diabetes, so his wound should be monitored closely. The 15cm incision is closed with 20 clips and is currently clean and dry under an Opsite Post-Op dressing. The clips are due for removal between 21 and 23 March. He has been prescribed enoxaparin 40mg subcutaneously once daily until 13 April. His wife has been taught to administer the injections; however, she is not yet confident and would benefit from supervision. For pain, he takes regular paracetamol 1g four times daily and codeine 30mg as required. He is mobilising with a walking frame indoors and two crutches for short distances. He must follow hip precautions for six weeks, avoiding bending beyond 90 degrees, crossing his legs and twisting. Both he and his wife have been advised about the signs of wound infection and deep vein thrombosis. Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me. Yours sincerely, Charge Nurse Ward 9 Orthopaedics Ashdown General Hospital

Why the model letter works

  • The opening paragraph states the reason for the letter (discharge after hip replacement) and exactly what the district nurse must do: wound review, clip removal and injection support.
  • Paragraphs follow the district nurse's tasks: wound and clips first, then medication and injections, then mobility and safety advice.
  • Diabetes is mentioned because it affects wound healing; old history such as the 1978 appendicectomy is left out.
  • The resolved temperature on day 2 is a distractor – it settled without treatment and does not change the community plan.
  • Hobbies (bowls), the son in Canada, driving advice and transport times are not needed by the district nurse.
  • Useful phrases: 'is due for removal between…', 'would benefit from supervision', 'has been advised about the signs of…'.

Frequently asked questions

How long should an OET nursing letter be?

The body of the letter should be about 180–200 words. The word count is a guide: assessors look at whether the letter contains the information the reader needs and leaves out what they do not need.

Which notes should I include?

Include the information the reader needs to continue care: the reason for writing, relevant diagnosis and treatment, current condition, social factors that affect care, and clear requests. Leave out resolved old history and personal details that do not affect care.
