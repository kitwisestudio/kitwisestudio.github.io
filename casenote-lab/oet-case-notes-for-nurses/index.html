Ms Fiona Grant Practice Nurse Hawthorn Medical Practice 22 Mill Lane Kettering NN15 6TQ 12 January 2026 Dear Ms Grant, Re: Leo Dawson, DOB 03/05/2017 I am writing to request that you review the umbilical wound of Leo Dawson, an eight-year-old boy who is being discharged home today, and remove his sutures on 19 January. Leo was admitted on 8 January with acute appendicitis and underwent a laparoscopic appendicectomy the following day. The appendix was found to be gangrenous but had not perforated. He received intravenous antibiotics for 48 hours and is now taking oral co-amoxiclav 250/62 mg suspension, 5 mL three times daily, for a further five days. His pain is well controlled with regular paracetamol and ibuprofen, which may be given as required after 48 hours. Although he has mild asthma, he has tolerated ibuprofen without any problems. Two port sites closed with tissue glue are healing well. However, the umbilical wound, which has non-absorbable sutures, shows mild erythema without discharge. A wound swab was taken yesterday and the result is pending. His parents have been taught to recognise the signs of wound infection. Leo should stay off school for one week and avoid contact sports and physical education for four weeks. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you have any queries. Yours sincerely, Staff Nurse Ward 12 (Paediatric Surgery), Northfield General Hospital

Address the letter to Ms Hannah Lowther, Health Visitor, Ashby Lane Health Centre, 22 Ashby Lane, Hollinsford HF4 2JD. Ms Hannah Lowther Health Visitor Ashby Lane Health Centre 22 Ashby Lane Hollinsford HF4 2JD 14 January 2026 Dear Ms Lowther, Re: Oscar Penhale, DOB 08/10/2025 I am writing to request a home visit within one week for Oscar, who is being discharged today after treatment for bronchiolitis, so that his feeding, weight and his mother's wellbeing can be reviewed. Oscar was admitted on 10 January with a two-day history of coryza, cough, breathlessness and reduced feeding. Following brief oxygen therapy and nasogastric feeding, he is now taking full feeds of 120 mL of formula three-hourly. However, his weight fell from 5.6 kg on admission to 5.5 kg, so regular weighing would be appreciated. His mother, Chloe, is a 19-year-old first-time parent who lives alone with Oscar. During the admission, she appeared anxious and tearful, slept poorly and said she finds it hard to cope when he is unwell. She also smokes approximately ten cigarettes daily, so further support to stop would be beneficial. Chloe has been taught to use saline nose drops and gentle nasal suction before feeds. She knows to seek urgent help if Oscar takes less than half his usual feeds, has pauses in his breathing or develops blue lips. Finally, his 12-week immunisations, deferred during admission, are now due. Yours sincerely, Staff Nurse Robin Ward (Paediatrics) Kingsmere General Hospital
