Free OET Writing checker for nurses

Paste your letter into the CaseNote Lab checker and get instant feedback grouped by the six OET Writing criteria. It runs in your browser: your letter is not sent anywhere.

What it checks

CriterionAutomatic checks
PurposeReason for writing in the first sentence; a clear request to the reader
ContentIn practice cases: which key notes you included or missed
Conciseness & ClarityBody length vs. the 180–200 word guide; irrelevant notes included; very long sentences
Genre & StyleContractions, informal words, note form, abbreviations and symbols
Organisation & LayoutAddress, date, salutation, Re: line, paragraphs, matching closing
LanguageErrors common for non-native writers ("explain him", "since 3 days", "informations"), capitals, repeated words, US/UK mix

The checker gives practice feedback based on rules. It does not replace a teacher and does not predict an official OET score.
