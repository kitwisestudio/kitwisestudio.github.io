Common OET Writing mistakes nurses make

These are the problems the CaseNote Lab checker finds most often in practice letters, grouped by the six OET Writing criteria.

MistakeExampleBetter
Purpose hidden in paragraph 2"Mr Bell is a 72-year-old retired teacher who…""I am writing to refer Mr Bell, who requires daily wound care following his discharge today."
Irrelevant case notes"He enjoys golf and his son lives in Canada."Leave out details the reader does not need to continue care.
Note form"Wound ok. Mobilising w/ frame.""His wound is clean and dry, and he is mobilising with a walking frame."
Contractions"He's", "doesn't""He is", "does not"
Informal words"got better", "a lot of", "kids""improved", "considerable", "children"
Closing does not match"Dear Ms Doe … Yours faithfully""Dear Ms Doe … Yours sincerely"
Duration with "since""since 3 days""for 3 days" or "since 3 March"
Missing "to""explain him the injections""explain the injections to him"
Uncountable nouns"informations", "equipments""information", "equipment"
"advice" as a verb"I advice her to rest""I advised her to rest" (verb = advise)
"discuss about""we discussed about his diet""we discussed his diet"
