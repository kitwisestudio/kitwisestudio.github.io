Christmas planning checklist: a week-by-week countdown

Updated 2026-10-28 · KitWise Studio · Holiday printables

Start Christmas planning in early November: set the total budget and gift list first, then book travel and send cards by early December, plan the menu two weeks out, and leave only fresh food and wrapping for the last week. Writing it all in one planner (budget, gifts, menu, cards) is what stops the December rush.

Week-by-week countdown

WhenTo do
Early NovemberSet a total budget, write the gift list with a limit per person, check dates and who is hosting.
Mid NovemberBuy the big gifts (Black Friday falls on 27 November 2026), book travel, order cards or photos.
Late NovemberPlan advent activities, decorations, and the party or dinner guest list.
Early DecemberSend cards, finish online orders (shipping slows down mid-December), plan the menu.
Mid DecemberShop non-perishables, print games and place cards, wrap gifts as they arrive.
Last weekFresh food, final cleaning, set the table, charge cameras, relax.

Keep spending under control

Track every gift against its budget as you buy it, not after. A simple tracker with a column for budget and a column for actual spend shows overspending while you can still fix it.

Quick answers

When should I start planning for Christmas?

Early November is enough for most families: it leaves time to set a budget, catch November sales and send cards before shipping slows in December.

What should a Christmas planner include?

A total budget, a gift list with a budget per person, a card list, a menu and shopping list, and a countdown of tasks by week.

When is Black Friday 2026?

Friday, 27 November 2026, the day after US Thanksgiving (26 November).
