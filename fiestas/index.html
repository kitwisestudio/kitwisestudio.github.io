How to run Christmas bingo for a big group

  1. Print one different card per player (identical cards cause ties).
  2. Cut the caller sheet into slips and draw them from a bowl or hat.
  3. Decide the pattern before you start: one line, four corners or full card.
  4. Have small prizes ready, and play 2 or 3 rounds with different patterns.

How the left-right gift exchange works

Everyone sits in a circle holding a wrapped gift. One person reads a short Christmas story out loud; every time the word left or right comes up, everyone passes their gift in that direction. When the story ends, you keep the gift you are holding. It takes about 10 minutes and needs no rules explanation.
