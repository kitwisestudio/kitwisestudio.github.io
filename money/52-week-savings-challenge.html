The 52-week savings challenge: totals and the trap

Updated 2026-10-29 · KitWise Money Math

The 52-week challenge (save $1 in week 1, $2 in week 2, up to $52 in week 52) adds up to $1,378. The catch: the quarters are uneven, $91, $260, $429 and $598, so the biggest deposits land in November and December. Doing it in reverse ($52 first) front-loads the hard weeks.
