The cost of waiting to invest

Updated 2026-10-28 · KitWise Money Math

Investing $300 a month from age 25 to 65 at an assumed 7% grows to about $787,000. Starting one year later gives about $731,000. You skipped only $3,600 of deposits but end up about $57,000 poorer, because the first deposits have the longest time to compound.

More examples

  • $5 a day (about $152 a month) from 25 vs 35, at an assumed 7%: the gap at 65 is over $200,000.
  • $60 a month of subscriptions ($720 a year), invested instead at 7%: about $10,400 after 10 years, $31,300 after 20 and $73,200 after 30.
