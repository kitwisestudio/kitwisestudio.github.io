15 vs 30 year mortgage: what $400,000 really costs

Updated 2026-10-29 · KitWise Money Math

Borrowing $400,000 at 6.5%: a 30-year loan costs $2,528 a month and about $510,000 in interest, so you repay about $910,000. A 15-year loan costs $3,484 a month but only about $227,000 in interest: about $283,000 less. Principal and interest only.
