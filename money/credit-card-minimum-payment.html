Paying only the minimum on a $5,000 credit card

Updated 2026-10-29 · KitWise Money Math

A $5,000 balance at 22% APR, paying only a common minimum (interest plus 1% of the balance, never below $25), takes about 230 months, roughly 19 years, and costs about $8,100 in interest. Paying a fixed $200 a month clears it in 34 months with about $1,750 of interest.

Why minimums take so long

The minimum shrinks as the balance shrinks, so most of each payment goes to interest for years. A fixed payment keeps the same pressure on the principal until it is gone.
