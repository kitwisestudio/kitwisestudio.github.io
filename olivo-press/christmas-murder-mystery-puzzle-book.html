A Christmas murder mystery puzzle book for adults

Updated 2026-11-08 · Olivo Press

Who Did It? Christmas Edition by Olivo Press is a book of 60 festive murder mystery logic puzzles: 20 warm-up (6x6), 20 tricky (7x7) and 20 expert (8x8), each with exactly one solution and full solutions at the back. It is $12.99 in paperback and $21.99 in hardcover on Amazon. A Spanish edition, ¿Quién fue? Especial Navidad, is on Amazon.es.

What is inside

  • Christmas settings: a Christmas market, an ugly sweater party, the office Secret Santa, a snowed-in cabin, a gingerbread contest, a New Year's Eve gala and more.
  • Same rules as vol. 1: one person per row and column; the killer is the one left alone with the victim (how to solve them).
  • Pure logic, no guessing: every case is computer-checked to have a unique solution.

Who it is for

Adults who like sudoku, logic grids, cozy mysteries or true crime: a stocking stuffer or a Secret Santa gift under $15.
