Large print word search book for seniors

Updated 2026-11-08 · Olivo Press

Large Print Word Search for Seniors by Olivo Press uses extra large letters set in Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed for low-vision readers, with one puzzle per page, words that only run forward and full solutions. It costs $9.99 in paperback and $19.99 in hardcover on Amazon.

What makes a word search easy to read

  • Big letters and clear grids (15x15) on 8.5 x 11 in pages.
  • A legible typeface: Atkinson Hyperlegible was designed by the Braille Institute for readers with low vision.
  • Words only forward, never backwards, so it stays relaxing.
  • One theme per page and solutions at the back.

A puzzle book for entertainment; it makes no medical claims.

Quick answers

What is a good puzzle book for someone with poor eyesight?

A large print book with one puzzle per page, big clear letters and words that run only forward.

What font is easiest to read for low vision?

Typefaces designed for legibility, such as Atkinson Hyperlegible from the Braille Institute, help tell similar letters apart.
