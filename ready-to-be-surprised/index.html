Ready To Be Surprised

Facts that sound fake, explained with real numbers. Space, money, history and everyday things, all calculated and shown on screen.

Watch on YouTube: @readytobesurprised. New Shorts most days and one longer video every week.

How the videos are made

Every number in a video is calculated, and every assumption is shown on screen. Charts and graphics are drawn by our own software. Narration uses a synthetic (text-to-speech) voice. Sources are listed in each video's description. Money videos are educational, not financial advice.

Uploader app

"Ready To Be Surprised Uploader" is a private tool the channel uses to upload its own videos and read its own statistics through the official YouTube API. It is not offered to other users. See the privacy policy.
