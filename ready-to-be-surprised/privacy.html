Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy covers "Ready To Be Surprised Uploader" (the "App"), the internal tool the YouTube channel Ready To Be Surprised uses through the YouTube API Services.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the owner of the Ready To Be Surprised channel, to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What data the App accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), the App accesses:

  • the owner's YouTube channel, to upload, schedule and update the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions;
  • YouTube Analytics reports for that channel (views, watch time, audience retention), to improve future videos.

The App does not access the data of other YouTube users, does not collect viewers' personal data, and does not use cookies or tracking.

How data is used, stored and shared

Data is used only to operate the channel. OAuth tokens and analytics reports are stored privately by the channel owner. They are not sold or shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

The App uses the YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, stored tokens stop working and are deleted.
