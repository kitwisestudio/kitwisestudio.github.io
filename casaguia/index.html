CasaGuía: a guest welcome book in 4 languages in about 10 minutes

Updated 2026-10-28 · CasaGuía

CasaGuía is an offline app (one HTML file) that builds a print-ready guest welcome book for a holiday rental in English, Spanish, German and French at once. You fill one form and get the book (A5 or A4), Wi-Fi QR cards and house rules, check-out and recycling signs. One-time price of €19, no account or subscription.

What the book covers

  • Welcome and host contact, Wi-Fi with a scan-to-connect QR code.
  • Check-in, check-out, address with a map QR code, how to get in, parking.
  • House rules, appliances, rubbish and recycling, emergency numbers.
  • Your local recommendations, a check-out list and a thank-you page.

Why 4 languages

Standard texts (rules, recycling, check-out, emergencies) are already translated, so guests from the UK, Germany, France and Spain each read the book in their language. Your own notes are written once and can be translated if you want.

Free version

Want to try it first? The free CasaGuía kit (ZIP, offline) prints Wi-Fi QR cards and a house rules sign in English, Spanish, German and French. No account or email needed.

Quick answers

Does CasaGuía work without internet?

Yes. It is a single HTML file that runs offline in Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox; your data stays on your computer.

Which languages does CasaGuía support?

English, Spanish, German and French, all generated at the same time.

Is CasaGuía an Airbnb product?

No. It works for hosts on Airbnb, Booking.com, VRBO or direct bookings, but it is not affiliated with any of them.
