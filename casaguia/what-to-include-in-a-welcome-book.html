What to include in a vacation rental welcome book

Updated 2026-10-27 · CasaGuía

A good vacation rental welcome book answers the questions guests ask most, in the order they need them: how to get in, the Wi-Fi, house rules, how appliances work, rubbish and recycling, emergency numbers, local recommendations and check-out steps. If many guests are international, offer it in their languages.

Checklist

  1. Arrival: address, map link or QR, parking, how to get the keys, check-in time.
  2. Wi-Fi: network and password, ideally a QR code to connect.
  3. House rules: quiet hours, smoking, pets, parties, maximum guests.
  4. Appliances: heating and air conditioning, washing machine, hob, coffee machine.
  5. Rubbish and recycling: which bin for what and where the containers are.
  6. Emergencies: 112 in the EU, nearest pharmacy and health centre, host contact.
  7. Local tips: food shops, restaurants, beaches, transport.
  8. Check-out: time, what to do with keys, towels, dishes and rubbish.

Tips

  • Keep one topic per page so guests find answers fast.
  • Put a Wi-Fi QR card and the rules on the wall too: many guests never open the book.
  • Check local rules for tourist rentals; some regions require specific information to be displayed.

What should be in an Airbnb welcome book?

Arrival and access, Wi-Fi, house rules, appliance instructions, rubbish and recycling, emergency numbers, local recommendations and check-out steps.

What is the EU emergency number?

112 works free of charge across the European Union.
