House rules for a holiday rental in 4 languages (free to copy)

Updated 2026-10-27 · CasaGuía

Here are 8 common holiday rental house rules written in English, Spanish, German and French, free to copy into your listing or welcome book: check-in and check-out times, no smoking, no parties, quiet hours, registered guests only, recycling, energy saving and the 112 emergency number. Adjust times and rules to your property and local regulations.

Tips

  • Keep rules short and positive; guests read signs, not paragraphs.
  • Use the same rules in your listing, welcome book and a wall sign.
  • Quiet hours and occupancy limits may be set by local rules or your building: check them.

Quick answers

How do I say 'no parties' in German and French?

German: 'Partys und Veranstaltungen sind nicht erlaubt.' French: 'Les fêtes et événements ne sont pas autorisés.'

What house rules should a holiday rental have?

At least check-in and check-out times, smoking, parties, quiet hours, maximum guests, rubbish and recycling, and an emergency number.
