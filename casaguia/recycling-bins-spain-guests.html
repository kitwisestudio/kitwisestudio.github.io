Which recycling bin is which in Spain? (for guests)

Updated 2026-11-03 · CasaGuía

In Spain: yellow = plastic packaging, cans and drink cartons; blue = paper and cardboard; green (igloo) = glass bottles and jars; brown = food waste, in towns that collect it; grey (or dark green) = everything else. Check your local council, because the brown bin is not in every town yet.

Quick table

Quick answers

Where do drink cartons go in Spain?

In the yellow bin, with plastic packaging and cans.

Where does glass go in Spain?

In the green igloo bin. Drinking glasses, mirrors and ceramics do not go there.

Is there a brown bin everywhere in Spain?

Not yet. Law 7/2022 requires separate collection of bio-waste, but rollout varies by town: check your local council.
