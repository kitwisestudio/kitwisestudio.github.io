Questions

What is the Be Ready campaign?
Be Ready is a UK government campaign launched by the Cabinet Office on 22 October 2026. It asks households to be ready to manage for 3 days during an emergency such as a power cut, a water outage, flooding or a cyber attack, and gives a checklist of supplies.
How much water do I need for 3 days?
The official checklist says 3 litres per person per day for drinking, so 9 litres per person for 3 days. A family of four needs 36 litres, which is 18 bottles of 2 litres. You need more for washing and cooking.
Do I have to buy everything at once?
No. The government advises building the kit gradually, picking up an item or two on regular shopping trips. Ready72 splits your list into 4 weeks and puts the free steps first.
How much does a 72-hour kit cost?
For two adults and two children the planner estimates around £170 at budget supermarket prices, and less if you already have blankets, a torch or a first aid kit. Prices are estimates.
Is my data stored anywhere?
No. Your household and ticks are saved only in your own browser. Sharing creates a link that contains only the household numbers.
