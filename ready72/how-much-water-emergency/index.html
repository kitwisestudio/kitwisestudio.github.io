How much water to store for a 72-hour emergency

Work out your own kit in 1 minute: free Ready72 planner

The UK Be Ready checklist says 3 litres per person per day for drinking, and more for cooking and washing. For 3 days that is 9 litres per person.

Pets and babies

Ready72 adds about 1.5 litres a day per dog and 0.3 litres a day per cat. For babies, the checklist recommends ready-to-feed formula, because you may not be able to boil water safely to make up powdered formula.

How to store it

  • Keep sealed bottles somewhere cool and dark, off the floor.
  • Write the date on the pack and swap it at your 6-month check.
  • If you fill your own bottles, use clean food-grade containers and replace the water every 6 months.
  • If a water outage is announced, fill the bath and pans for washing and flushing.

Questions

How much water does a family of four need for 3 days?
36 litres for drinking, which is 18 bottles of 2 litres, following the UK Be Ready figure of 3 litres per person per day.
Is tap water OK to store?
Yes, in clean food-grade containers with tight lids. Replace it every 6 months.
