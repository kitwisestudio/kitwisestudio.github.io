Your 2026, recapped in 60 seconds

Answer 12 quick questions and get your own year-in-review story cards, ready for Instagram, TikTok and WhatsApp. Free, no sign-up, nothing uploaded.


How it works

  1. Answer 12 quick questions about your year. Skip anything you like.
  2. We turn your answers into 7 story cards (1080×1920) and one feed post, plus your 2026 archetype.
  3. Share them straight to your story, or send friends a link so they can see your year and make their own.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Making and downloading your cards is free, with no account and no watermark beyond a small YearCard credit at the bottom.

Is this connected to Spotify, Instagram or TikTok?

No. YearCard is an independent tool. It does not read any of your accounts. It only uses what you type.

Where do my answers go?

Nowhere. The cards are drawn in your own browser. Your answers are saved only on your device so you can come back. If you copy your share link, that link contains your answers.

What are the 2026 archetypes?

There are 8: The Explorer, The Builder, The Main Character, The Quiet Glow-Up, The Heart, The Soft Life Pro, The Phoenix and The Wildcard. Yours depends on where your energy went, your numbers and your vibe.
