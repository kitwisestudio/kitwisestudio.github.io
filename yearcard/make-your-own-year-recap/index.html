How to make your own year-in-review story

Every December, music and social apps release their yearly recaps, and everybody posts theirs. Those recaps only cover what you streamed or watched. Here's how to make one about your actual life, for free, in about a minute.

Make your YearCard (free)

Option 1: YearCard (fastest, free)

  1. Open YearCard and press start.
  2. Answer 12 quick questions: your word of the year, how the year felt, where your energy went, your numbers, your song, best moment, something hard you got through, a lesson, your biggest win and your 2027 era.
  3. You get 7 story cards in 1080×1920 and a 4:5 feed post, plus your 2026 archetype (one of 8).
  4. On a phone, press “Share my cards” to send them straight to Instagram, TikTok or WhatsApp. On a computer, download them.
  5. Copy your recap link and send it to friends so they can see your year and make theirs.

Option 2: build it by hand in a design app

  1. Start a 1080×1920 story design.
  2. Make one slide per stat: one big number or phrase, one short label.
  3. Use 2 bold colours and one font, big.
  4. Keep each slide readable in 3 seconds; stories move fast.
  5. Export as PNG and post in order.

What to put in a personal recap

  1. Your 2026 word
  2. A year rating out of 5
  3. New places, books, people and events
  4. Song of your year
  5. Best moment
  6. What you got through
  7. What the year taught you
  8. Biggest win
  9. Your 2027 era

Is YearCard official?

No. YearCard is an independent free tool and is not affiliated with Spotify, Instagram, TikTok or any app. It doesn't connect to your accounts and doesn't upload your answers.
